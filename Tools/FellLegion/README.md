# FellLegion.rte generators

The Fell Legion's art, sounds and actor definitions are made by these scripts on top of `Tools/Medieval`'s (the Legion is the kingdom's dark mirror, so most of its drawings are the Medieval grids in black iron, crimson, purple and sickly green).

- `sprites.py <Data folder>`: the units' torsos and heads (Medieval's grids swapped to the Legion's colours, plus the Dread Lord's horned helm, the Executioner's hood, the Lich's skull, the Skeleton Warrior's bones, the Ghoul's grey-green hide and the Imp's horned red head), their limbs (Ronin's, recoloured), the weapons (black steel versions of the Medieval ones, and the Legion's own Executioner's axe, barbed flail, bone javelin, rusted sword and bone shield), the pots, the module icon, the spells' books, scrolls, orbs, rune circle and glows, and the Hellhound's sprites (the Base Hound's, reshaded black with ember eyes, into `Actors/Hellhound/`).
- `actors.py <Data folder>`: writes `Actors.ini` with `Tools/Medieval/actors.py`'s writer (`MODULE = "FellLegion"`): fifteen humanoid units, every one in the Unholy group (the Hellhound, a crab, is `Actors/Hellhound/Hellhound.ini`, by hand).
- `sounds.py <Data folder>`: synthesises `Sounds/` as FLAC with `Tools/Medieval/spellsounds.py`'s helpers (needs numpy and ffmpeg): the Doom's toll and burst, the chains, the shadow bolt, the life drain, the bone throw, the hellfire's fall, and the second book's hex zap, curse whisper, fear pulse, blood pact, shadow step, darkness fall, dark chime, soul wisp and ghoul rise.
- `shaders.py <Data folder>`: writes the status effects' unit shaders (`Shaders/Hexed.frag`, `Cursed.frag`, `Frenzied.frag`, `Burning.frag`) with `Tools/Medieval/shaders.py`'s writer from `Base.rte`'s Frozen.frag. `Shaders/Darkness.frag` (the fullscreen night) and `Shaders/Shaders.ini` are by hand.

Run from the repository root: `python3 Tools/FellLegion/sprites.py Data`, and the same for the others. `Index.ini`, `Devices.ini`, `Devices/Arsenal/Arsenal.ini`, `Devices/Spells/Spells.ini`, `Loadouts.ini`, `Sounds.ini`, `Speech/FellLegion.ini`, `Actors/Hellhound/Hellhound.ini`, `Shaders/Shaders.ini`, `Shaders/Darkness.frag` and the Lua scripts are written by hand.
