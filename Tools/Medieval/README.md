# Medieval.rte generators

The Medieval faction's art, sounds and actor definitions are made by these scripts, so a change to a unit is a change here and a re-run, not a hand edit of 60 PNGs.

- `sprites.py <Data folder>`: the torsos, heads, weapons, shields, arrows and module icon are ASCII grids in the script (the Longbowman, Hunter, Arbalester, Royal Guard and Alchemist included); the arms, legs, feet and hands are Ronin's, recoloured onto each unit's colours so their frames and joints still fit. Everything is saved in the game palette.
- `sounds.py <Data folder>`: synthesises the swings, clashes, cuts, bow and crossbow shots, bow draw and arrow hits as FLAC (needs numpy and ffmpeg).
- `buildings.py <Data folder>`: draws the buildings (huts, cottages, a longhouse, a stone house, a watchtower, tents, a market stall, a palisade, thatched roofs) and writes `Scenes/Buildings/Buildings.ini`. Each is a terrain object in the build menu's Bunker Modules (and its own Medieval Buildings group), made of Wood, Stone and the module's own Thatch, Canvas and Daub (`Materials.ini`).
- `actors.py <Data folder>`: writes `Actors.ini` (each unit's head, limbs and body, and the leap they have instead of a jetpack).
- `spells.py <Data folder>`: draws the spells (`Devices/Spells`): each spell's book, tome, scroll or stone tablet as it lies on the ground (its own cover colour and emblem, so they can be told apart), the fireball, meteor and poison and gravity orbs, the rune circles, the props the second batch of spells leaves on the field (the stone spike and wall, the bridge of light, the ice crust), and the coloured glows (true-colour RGB, black for nothing) the spells' ScreenEffects use.
- `spellsounds.py <Data folder>`: synthesises the spells' sounds into `Sounds/Spells` as FLAC (needs numpy and ffmpeg): casting whooshes, the fireball's roar and blast, the dragon's breath loop, thunder, shattering ice, arcane zings, the nova, the summoning, wind, rumbling earth, the meteor's fall, the vortex hum, the poison burst, the time warp's ticking warble, the support spells' chimes and shimmers, and the second batch's chain zap, ice lance, blizzard, holy smite, sunbeam, water jet, wolf howl, stone spikes, thunderclap and rising wall.

Run from the repository root: `python3 Tools/Medieval/sprites.py Data`, and the same for the others. `Devices.ini`, `Sounds.ini`, `Loadouts.ini` and `Materials.ini` are written by hand, and so are the spells' `Devices/Spells/Spells.ini`, their Lua and `Shaders/`.
- `arsenal.py <Data folder>`: draws the arsenal in `Devices/Arsenal` (firepot, stinkpot, Holy Hand Grenade, throwing hammer, bolas, caltrops and their shards; warhammer, mace, halberd, greatsword; the warbow, hunting bow and arbalest, flamberge, poleaxe, flail, falchion, morning star, hunting knife, war scythe and quarterstaff, tower shield and buckler, throwing spear, francisca, sling and its stones, net and snare trap, and the oil, frost and smoke flasks and healing draught) as ASCII grids in the game palette, and its coloured RGB glows (wick, fire, stink, holy, frost, draught). `Devices/Arsenal/Arsenal.ini`, `Arsenal2.ini` and their Lua scripts are written by hand.
- `arsenalsounds.py <Data folder>`: synthesises `Sounds/Arsenal` as FLAC: pots shattering, greek fire's whoosh and roar, the stinkpot's burst, the bolas' whirr and wrap, caltrops scattering and biting, heavy blunt hits and swings, the Holy Hand Grenade's "Hallelujah" choir and blast, the warbow's and arbalest's shots, the sling's whirr and its stones hitting, the net wrapping, the snare trap snapping, the flail whirling, and the frost flask's and healing draught's bursts (needs numpy and ffmpeg).
- `shaders.py <Data folder>`: writes the status effects' object shaders (`Shaders/*.frag`) from `Frozen.frag`, the hand-written one; `TimeWarp.frag` and `Blizzard.frag`, the screen passes, are written by hand.
- `creatures.py <Data folder>`: draws the faction's creatures (`Actors/Wolf`) from Base.rte's creature rigs, recoloured onto the creature's own shades so the rig's frames and joints still fit. `Actors/Wolf/Wolf.ini` is written by hand.

Each script's `main()` runs only when the script is run itself, so `Tools/FellLegion`'s generators import these for their grids, palettes and writers; `actors.py`'s `MODULE` is the module it writes (`Medieval` here).
