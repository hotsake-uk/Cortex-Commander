# Medieval.rte generators

The Medieval faction's art, sounds and actor definitions are made by these scripts, so a change to a unit is a change here and a re-run, not a hand edit of 60 PNGs.

- `sprites.py <Data folder>`: the torsos, heads, weapons, shields, arrows and module icon are ASCII grids in the script; the arms, legs, feet and hands are Ronin's, recoloured onto each unit's colours so their frames and joints still fit. Everything is saved in the game palette.
- `sounds.py <Data folder>`: synthesises the swings, clashes, cuts, bow and crossbow shots, bow draw and arrow hits as FLAC (needs numpy and ffmpeg).
- `buildings.py <Data folder>`: draws the buildings (huts, cottages, a longhouse, a stone house, a watchtower, tents, a market stall, a palisade, thatched roofs) and writes `Scenes/Buildings/Buildings.ini`. Each is a terrain object in the build menu's Bunker Modules (and its own Medieval Buildings group), made of Wood, Stone and the module's own Thatch, Canvas and Daub (`Materials.ini`).
- `actors.py <Data folder>`: writes `Actors.ini` (each unit's head, limbs and body, and the leap they have instead of a jetpack).
- `spells.py <Data folder>`: draws the spells (`Devices/Spells`): each spell's book, tome, scroll or stone tablet as it lies on the ground (its own cover colour and emblem, so they can be told apart), the fireball, meteor and poison and gravity orbs, the rune circles, and the coloured glows (true-colour RGB, black for nothing) the spells' ScreenEffects use.
- `spellsounds.py <Data folder>`: synthesises the spells' sounds into `Sounds/Spells` as FLAC (needs numpy and ffmpeg): casting whooshes, the fireball's roar and blast, the dragon's breath loop, thunder, shattering ice, arcane zings, the nova, the summoning, wind, rumbling earth, the meteor's fall, the vortex hum, the poison burst and the time warp's ticking warble.

Run from the repository root: `python3 Tools/Medieval/sprites.py Data`, and the same for the others. `Devices.ini`, `Sounds.ini`, `Loadouts.ini` and `Materials.ini` are written by hand, and so are the spells' `Devices/Spells/Spells.ini`, their Lua and `Shaders/`.
- `arsenal.py <Data folder>`: draws the arsenal in `Devices/Arsenal` (firepot, stinkpot, Holy Hand Grenade, throwing hammer, bolas, caltrops and their shards; warhammer, mace, halberd, greatsword) as ASCII grids in the game palette, and its coloured RGB glows (wick, fire, stink, holy). `Devices/Arsenal/Arsenal.ini` and its Lua scripts are written by hand.
- `arsenalsounds.py <Data folder>`: synthesises `Sounds/Arsenal` as FLAC: pots shattering, greek fire's whoosh and roar, the stinkpot's burst, the bolas' whirr and wrap, caltrops scattering and biting, heavy blunt hits and swings, and the Holy Hand Grenade's "Hallelujah" choir and blast (needs numpy and ffmpeg).
